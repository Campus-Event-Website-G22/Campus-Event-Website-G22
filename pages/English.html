About the Event

PNC Student Volleyball Match was an exciting sports event organized for PNC students to come together, compete, and support their teams. The event brought students from different classes together on the volleyball court, creating an energetic atmosphere filled with teamwork, competition, cheering, and school spirit.

What to Expect

  • Exciting volleyball matches between team
  • Teamwork and communication between players
  • Cheering and support from classmates
  • Celebration of the winning team

Who Should Attend

  • Volleyball players and team members
  • Classmates and friends of participating teams
  • Students who enjoy sports and school activities
  • Anyone who wants to support the PNC community
Important Note:Please arrive early to support the players and avoid missing the beginning of the match. Players should bring their appropriate sportswear, water, and any necessary volleyball equipment. Most importantly, come ready to cheer, have fun, and support your team!
